Introduction back to ToC

This ontology is part of the catalogue of ontologies being developed in the context of the Data Space for Smart Urban Infrastructures (EDINT). The EDINT ontology catalogue aims to provide reusable semantic models for the representation and publication of data related to urban infrastructures, municipal services and administrative processes managed by local entities. The ontology focuses on the representation of public services and administrative procedures offered by local administrations. Its scope includes the description of services provided by a municipality, the procedures through which citizens, companies or other agents interact with the administration, and the relationships between these procedures, the competent public organisations, the required inputs, the generated outputs and the applicable rules.
